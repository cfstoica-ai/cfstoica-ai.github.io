signalfeed-backup: privacy policy

signalfeed-backup is a personal backup tool used only by the owner of this site, with the owner's own Google account.

What Google data it uses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. That lets it create, read, change and delete only the files it created itself. It cannot see or open any other file in the Drive.

How the data is used and stored

The files it creates are encrypted backups of the owner's own server. They are encrypted before they leave the server, and they are stored in the owner's Google Drive. The sign-in token Google issues stays on the owner's server.

Sharing

The tool does not share the files with any other person or service, and does not sell, transfer or analyse any Google user data. Google stores the encrypted files as part of the owner's Drive.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google account's security settings, and can delete the backup files from Google Drive.
